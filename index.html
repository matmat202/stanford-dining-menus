This site publishes its menu feed and a drop-in widget, so a dorm page, club site or department intranet can show a dining hall without scraping R&DE. Two lines:

<div data-stanford-dining="Arrillaga"></div>
<script src="https://matmat202.github.io/stanford-dining-menus/widget.js" defer></script>

Which renders, live, right here:

Options: data-meal, data-diet (vg v gf h k), data-limit (default 8, 0 for all), data-theme, data-src. Halls by id or display name, case-insensitive. It shows Stanford's today, not the reader's, so an embed read from another timezone still says the right thing — and when it has nothing it says why rather than showing another day's menu.

Allergens. Ingredient and allergen data comes from Stanford R&DE and changes without notice. R&DE cannot guarantee the absence of allergens in its food or facilities — do not rely on this page for a medically significant allergy.
